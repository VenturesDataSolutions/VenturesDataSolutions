The Platform

Two product lines. Same idea: automate the busywork.

VDS builds two separate automation product lines for real estate — one for investors, one for agents. They’re priced and built independently, each with three tiers, so you only pay for the line that fits what you do.

For Investors

Expense tracking, research, and comps.

Text or photo an expense and it’s routed to the right property’s job-cost sheet automatically, with tax categories rolled up for you. Higher tiers add property research pulling public records, comps cross-referenced against your own job-cost history, and full document handling.

See Investor plans & pricing

For Agents

Pipeline automation, start to close.

Automated lead intake and follow-up reminders out of the gate. Higher tiers add showing schedule automation, listing status tracking, stage-based follow-up sequences, and support for multi-agent teams.

See Agent plans & pricing

Not the county subscription

Looking for county tax & probate data?

That’s a separate, standalone product — not part of either platform line above. See the County Data subscription for details.
