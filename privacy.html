Privacy Policy

Venture$ Data Solutions (“VDS,” “we,” “us,” or “our”)

Last updated: August 20, 2026.

This Privacy Policy describes how Venture$ Data Solutions collects, uses, discloses, and otherwise processes personal information in connection with our website and services (the “Services”).

This policy applies to information we collect through our website and related communications. It does not apply to information collected offline or through third-party sites or services that we do not control.

This is a draft document pending legal review. It is not legal advice.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you provide directly to us and information that is automatically collected when you use the Services.

Information You Provide

  • Contact and account information (e.g., name, email address, phone number if provided)
  • Communication content when you contact us or subscribe
  • Payment and billing information when online checkout is available (processed by our payment provider, Stripe; we do not store full payment card numbers)

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device and usage data (e.g., IP address, browser type, pages viewed, referring/exit pages, timestamps)
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Cookies section below)

Categories of Personal Information (for transparency under applicable U.S. state privacy laws)

We may collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Identifiers (name, email address, IP address, account identifiers)
  • Commercial information (subscription or purchase history, billing details)
  • Internet or other electronic network activity information (browsing and usage data on our site)
  • Professional or employment-related information (if you provide it in communications)
  • Inferences drawn from the above (limited, for service improvement)

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information as defined under applicable law (e.g., precise geolocation, racial or ethnic origin, health data, or biometric identifiers) unless you voluntarily provide it and we have a lawful basis to process it.

2. How We Use Personal Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Services and your subscription
  • Process transactions and manage billing
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Send administrative messages (e.g., service updates, security notices)
  • Improve, personalize, and develop our Services
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, or security incidents
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms
  • For other purposes with your consent or as otherwise permitted by law

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. How We Share Personal Information

We may share personal information in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers / processors: With vendors who perform services on our behalf (e.g., payment processing via Stripe, hosting, email delivery, analytics). These providers are contractually restricted from using the information for other purposes.
  • Legal and safety reasons: When required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of VDS, our users, or others.
  • Business transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of assets, in which case the information may be transferred as part of the transaction (subject to appropriate protections).
  • With your direction or consent: When you instruct us to share information or otherwise consent.

We do not sell personal information. We also do not “share” personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA) and similar state laws, based on our current practices.

Categories of Third Parties

Personal information may be disclosed to:

  • Payment processors (Stripe)
  • Cloud hosting and infrastructure providers
  • Email and communication service providers
  • Analytics and security vendors (if used)

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We and our service providers may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to operate the site, remember preferences, understand usage, and improve the Services. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect site functionality.

We do not currently respond to “Do Not Track” browser signals in a standardized way, as there is no consistent industry standard.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide the Services, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, comply with legal and accounting obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Retention periods vary depending on the type of data and the context. When information is no longer needed, we will delete or de-identify it in accordance with our data retention practices.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your place of residence, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information. These may include the right to:

  • Know / Access: Request information about the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you, and the categories of sources, purposes, and third parties with whom it is shared.
  • Delete: Request deletion of personal information we have collected from you, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Correct: Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Opt out of sale or sharing: Opt out of the sale of personal information or sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising (we do not currently sell or share in this manner).
  • Limit use of sensitive personal information: Limit the use of sensitive personal information if applicable (we do not currently process sensitive personal information for purposes that would trigger this right in most cases).
  • Non-discrimination: Not be discriminated against for exercising your privacy rights.

These rights are subject to applicable legal limitations and verification requirements. Residents of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, and other states with comprehensive privacy laws may have additional or more specific rights.

How to Exercise Your Rights

To submit a request, email us at hello@venturesdatasolutions.com with the subject line “Privacy Request” and include:

  • Your full name and the email address associated with your interactions with us
  • A description of the request
  • Sufficient information for us to verify your identity

We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law (generally 45 days for California residents, subject to extension). We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling certain requests. You may also authorize an agent to submit requests on your behalf; we will require proof of authorization.

If you are a California resident, you may also designate an authorized agent as provided under the CCPA/CPRA.

7. Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, use, alteration, or destruction. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. SMS/Text Messaging

We collect your phone number for SMS purposes only after you complete our SMS consent process at expense-intake.venturesdatasolutions.workers.dev/consent, where you enter your own number and expressly opt in. We do not add a phone number to our SMS program without that opt-in step.

We use your phone number to send you SMS text messages related to expense-tracking for VDS Expense Tracker — for example, expense confirmations, requests to identify which property an expense belongs to, and related account texts. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. You can opt out of these text messages at any time by replying “STOP” to any message, or request help by replying “HELP.”

9. Children’s Privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 16 (or under 13 where applicable), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

10. International Users

The Services are operated from the United States. If you access the Services from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States or other countries that may have different data protection laws than your country of residence. By using the Services, you acknowledge that your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and update the “Last updated” date. Material changes may be communicated by additional means (e.g., email or prominent notice) when required by law. Your continued use of the Services after the effective date of any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy where permitted by law.
