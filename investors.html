Worth keeping in mind

A single real estate deal often nets ten thousand dollars or more in profit. That’s not a promise about your results — every deal and market is different — but it’s the rough math worth keeping in mind: tools that help you close even one extra deal a year can pay for themselves several times over.

Handles messy input

Upload receipts, PDFs, contracts, or handwritten notes — the system reads and organizes them automatically. You don’t need clean, structured data to get started.

About the research data

Public property records, not exclusive leads.

Core and Full Investor Platform research pulls from general public property records — county auditor data, deed transfers, and closed comparable sales. This is separate from VDS’s county tax-delinquency & probate data subscription, which is a distinct, standalone product with its own exclusivity terms. The Full Investor Platform tier can integrate with that subscription if you’re already a subscriber, but the platform itself does not include or bundle exclusive lead data.
