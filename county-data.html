Why exclusivity holds up

It’s not just a promise — it’s how the subscription is structured.

When you subscribe to a county, that county is reserved to you at the process level, not just by policy: we don’t run or sell a second pull for a county that’s already locked to an active subscriber. If you cancel, the reservation is released and the county becomes available for the next subscriber to claim — there’s no bidding war and no surprise competitor buying in behind you while you’re active.

Not part of the platform products

This data isn’t included in the Investor or Agent platforms.

The tax-delinquency and probate leads in this subscription are sold only here, as a standalone product. The Investor and Agent automation platforms use general public property records (comps, closed sales, deed transfers) for their research features — if you’re on the Full Investor Platform tier and already have an active county subscription, the platform can integrate with it, but no platform tier includes or bundles this lead data on its own.
