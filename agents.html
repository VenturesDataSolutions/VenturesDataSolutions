About property data

General records, not exclusive leads.

Where the platform touches property data at all, it draws on general public records — closed comparable sales and listing status — not the exclusive tax-delinquency and probate leads sold separately through VDS’s county data subscription. That subscription is a distinct, standalone product and isn’t bundled into any Agent Platform tier.
